# Glossary

- **Shard** – the single harvestable crystal resource; spent as credits.
- **Credits** – a player's banked shards.
- **Hauler** – the unit that carries shards from a shard field to a refinery (Shard Hauler, Gorger). _Avoid_: harvester.
- **Hopper** – the Shard Hauler's load box: it fills in plain sight as the hauler mines and tips up to pour the load out at the dock pad. _Avoid_: cargo, bed.
- **Home field** – a shard field beside a player's start; every start has two.
- **Natural field** – the shard field nearest a base after its two home fields, the first one it expands to.
- **Dock pad** – the one place at a refinery where a hauler unloads; other haulers wait their turn beside it.
- **Tick** – one fixed simulation step; game time is counted in ticks (20 to a game second).
- **Game speed** – how fast game time passes in real time (×0.8 by default); it never changes what happens in the game.
- **Fly-in** – the camera sweep that opens every match, from a view of the whole map down to the player's base; game time starts only when it ends. _Avoid_: intro, opening cinematic.
- **Snapshot** – an immutable, per-player, fog-limited view of the game at a tick.
- **Command** – a player or AI instruction submitted to the simulation (move, build, attack…).
- **Acknowledgement** – the immediate sight and sound shown to the player who selects a unit or gives a command, before any command takes effect; seen only by that player, never part of the simulation.
- **Seam** – the public simulation API through which everything else interacts with it.
- **State hash** – a deterministic hash of the full game state, used to prove determinism.
- **Faction** – Humans (the Aegis Union) or Aliens (the Vrax Hive); Aegis and Vrax for short.
- **Map** – the battlefield a skirmish is played on, chosen before the game: a hand-made map (Crossroads, Riverbed or Highlands) or a generated map; every map is the same for both players, turned half a turn.
- **Generated map** – a map produced from a map seed; the same seed always gives the same map.
- **Map seed** – the number that identifies a generated map; shown so a map can be replayed or shared.
- **Tier** – depth in the tech tree (1–3).
- **Power grid** – total power produced versus consumed; a deficit slows production and disables power-hungry buildings.
- **Fog of war** – per-player visibility: hidden, explored (remembered), visible.
- **Superweapon** – a faction's long-charging area-effect weapon.
- **Control group** – a numbered (1–9) set of units the player can recall with one key.
- **Scatter** – an order that sends each selected unit a few tiles straight away from the group's centre, spreading a bunched army.
- **Attack-move** – an order to travel to a point and engage any enemy met on the way.
- **Rally point** – where a production building sends newly built units.
- **Combat unit** – any unit that can attack; haulers are not combat units.
- **Infantry squad** – a unit made of several foot soldiers that move, fight and die as one (Rifleman, Rocketeer, Skitterer, Spitter). _Avoid_: infantry blob, troop.
- **Crusher** – a heavy unit that kills enemy infantry squads by driving over them (Bulwark Tank, Brute, and both heroes; a hero also crushes vehicles).
- **Steamroll** – the strategy of out-producing the enemy and driving one large mixed army, built around crushers, forward as one body, the crushers flattening infantry squads on the way. _Avoid_: tank spam, tank rush.
- **Hero** – a faction's one giant unit (the Paladin or the Dreadstrider): a durable meat-shield tank, at most one per player at a time. _Avoid_: epic unit.
- **Unit kind** – what a unit is (Rifleman, Bulwark Tank, Brute…). "Type" is reserved for damage types and armour types.
- **Difficulty** – how well the AI plays (Easy, Medium, Hard): its reaction time, actions per minute and resource bonus; never its vision or the rules.
- **Personality** – the strategy an AI opponent commits to for a match (Raider, Balanced or Doomsday); chosen independently of Difficulty, and changes only what the AI chooses to do.
- **Staging point** – where an AI gathers its army before sending it out, a short way from its headquarters toward the enemy.
- **Wave** – an AI army sent out together from the staging point to attack. _Avoid_: push, attack group.
- **Standing army** – an AI's combat units that are not in a wave, a raid or scouting: the units holding at home.
- **Harasser** – an enemy force that hits units and pulls away faster than those units can catch it, such as Glare Skimmers, or Bulwark Tanks backing off as they fire. _Avoid_: raider, kiter.
- **Army ball** – an army kept close around its centre so it fights as one body and no unit strays out alone. _Avoid_: blob, clump, deathball.
- **Strike group** – a small AI group of fast combat units sent to raid apart from the main army. _Avoid_: hit squad (a squad is an infantry squad).
- **Retreat** – an AI pulling an army back toward friendly strength because the enemy facing it is too strong.
- **Fighting retreat** – a retreat by an army that cannot outrun its pursuers, so it keeps facing them and firing as it falls back.
- **Regroup** – retreated units and reinforcements gathering into one army at friendly strength before going out again.
- **Concave** – an army drawn up in an arc or line facing the enemy, so every unit can fire and a charging crusher meets the whole line. _Avoid_: formation, wall.
- **Watchpost** – a neutral building on the map that infantry squads can garrison for cover, sight and a place to fire from; held by whoever is inside. _Avoid_: garrison (the Aegis barracks), bunker.
