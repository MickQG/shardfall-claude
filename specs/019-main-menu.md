# 019 Main menu (milestone 19)

A modern start screen: a left rail of modes and a panel for the chosen one, over a slowly drifting backdrop.

- **MENU-1**: The main menu is a full-screen layout inside the game frame: a left rail with the title and four entries (Single player, Multiplayer, Units, Settings) and, to the right, the panel of the chosen mode. Single player shows the two faction cards (each naming its hero), the opponent (Easy, Medium, Hard) as one segmented control, one card per map with a thumbnail drawn from the map itself (ground, rock, water, shard fields and both starts) and its description, and START; Enter starts the game and Up and Down move through the rail. The backdrop drifts slowly, and holds still when the system asks for reduced motion.
- **MENU-2**: Multiplayer opens the online panel (NET-1): host a room or join one with a code; where online play is unavailable the panel says why and the rail marks Multiplayer "Unavailable".
- **MENU-3**: The Settings panel sets graphics quality (High or Low), volume and mute; changes are saved on the device at once and the next game uses them. Units opens the unit info sheet for the selected faction (INF-1..4).
- **MENU-4**: On the Single player panel a "View units" button sits on the same row as the opponent selector, at its right-hand end; it opens the unit sheet for the picked faction (INF-1, INF-6).
- **MENU-7**: The Aegis faction card's hero line reads exactly "Paladin, a giant assault walker with cannons and missiles." and fits on one row of the card at desktop and phone widths. The Vrax line is unchanged. (Numbered 7 because MENU-6 is the Generated map card, spec 024.)
