# 029 Unit restyle (milestone 29)

Issue #170: the three Aegis vehicles (Shard Hauler, Bulwark Tank, Hammer Artillery) become grounded science fiction, the hauler's hopper tips further and no longer clips the refinery; the amended R3D-22, R3D-23, R3D-30 and R3D-32 are in their own files.

- **R3D-35**: Aegis vehicles share one look: grounded science fiction. The Shard Hauler, Bulwark Tank and Hammer Artillery run on wheels or tracks and stand on the ground with weight, in sealed, faceted armour panels, with no exhaust stacks, exposed hydraulic rams, hatches, stowage or visible crew; power vents, breeches and sensors carry small cyan-lit details. They match the Scout Buggy (R3D-33) and never look more advanced than the Paladin. Each keeps its footprint, overall size, team-coloured parts, role silhouette and muzzle points, so picking, build pictures and shots are unchanged; what their guns fire is unchanged. Every one is an original design.
- **R3D-36**: The Bulwark Tank is a low, wide battle tank. Its tracks run under full-length armoured skirts with only their lower run showing. A flat, wedge-shaped turret with no hatches sits mid-hull and carries one heavy cannon: a thick barrel in a ribbed accelerator sleeve with a cyan-lit breech. A sensor blister and a short blade antenna stand where a cupola would; a lit power vent sits at the rear. Green panels, with team colour on the skirts and the turret plate.
- **PERF-7**: Restyled models stay within a triangle limit. The Bulwark Tank and the Brute, which fill the 200-unit brawl, use no more triangles than before the restyle (counted for the model and for its shadow stand-in), and the brawl measures no slower. The Shard Hauler and the Hammer Artillery, of which few are on screen, use at most twice as many as before.
