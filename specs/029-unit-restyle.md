# 029 Unit restyle (milestone 29)

Issue #170: the three Aegis vehicles (Shard Hauler, Bulwark Tank, Hammer Artillery) become grounded science fiction, the hauler's hopper tips further and no longer cuts into the refinery, and the Brute is rebuilt with real joints and a real claw blow; a rule now keeps every walking unit in one piece. The amended R3D-22, R3D-23, R3D-30 and R3D-32 stay in their own files. 3D view only: nothing in the simulation or the unit data changes.

- **R3D-35**: Aegis vehicles share one look: grounded science fiction. The Shard Hauler, Bulwark Tank and Hammer Artillery run on wheels or tracks and stand on the ground with weight, in sealed, faceted armour panels, with no exhaust stacks, exposed hydraulic rams, hatches, stowage or visible crew; power vents, breeches and sensors carry small cyan-lit details. They match the Scout Buggy (R3D-33) and never look more advanced than the Paladin. Each keeps its footprint, overall size, team-coloured parts, role silhouette and muzzle points, so picking, build pictures and shots are unchanged; what their guns fire is unchanged. Every one is an original design.
- **R3D-36**: The Bulwark Tank is a low, wide battle tank. Its tracks run under full-length armoured skirts with only their lower run showing. A flat, wedge-shaped turret with no hatches sits mid-hull and carries one heavy cannon: a thick barrel in a ribbed accelerator sleeve with a cyan-lit breech. A sensor blister and a short blade antenna stand where a cupola would; a lit power vent sits at the rear. Green panels, with team colour on the skirts and the turret plate.
- **R3D-37**: The Brute is a hulking, hunched Vrax bruiser that walks upright on two thick legs: a heavy torso under a team-coloured armoured back with a hump of bone spikes, a low, tusked head set between massive shoulders, glowing green eyes and veins, and two long, heavy arms ending in three-clawed fists. Each arm bends at shoulder, elbow and wrist and each leg at hip, knee and ankle; every joint is a rounded knuckle that the limb segments sink into. It walks with weight, arms swinging against the legs. It strikes with claw blows: each attack drives one arm forward and down, alternating arms, the torso twisting into it, the blow landing with the damage; the legs stay planted and joined to the body at the hips throughout.
- **AIM-4**: Parts never come apart. On every walking unit, in every pose AIM-2 lists and in every attack pose, each limb segment meets the next, or a joint piece covers where they meet, and each limb meets the body: no gap wider than 0.03 tiles shows at any joint.
- **PERF-7**: Restyled models stay within a triangle limit. The Bulwark Tank and the Brute, which fill the 200-unit brawl, use no more triangles than before the restyle (counted for the model and for its shadow stand-in), and a frame's draw calls stay within the limit that R3D-13's test sets. The Shard Hauler and the Hammer Artillery, of which few are on screen, use at most twice as many as before.
