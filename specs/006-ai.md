# 006 AI opponent and harness (milestone 6)

The AI is an ordinary client of the simulation: it reads a fog-limited snapshot of its own player, plus the static map, and returns ordinary commands.

- **AI-1**: The AI builds a working economy and base unaided: power, refineries, haulers, production buildings and defences, advancing through the tech tree.
- **AI-2**: The AI has no vision cheat: changing a hidden part of the world that the AI cannot see never changes the commands it issues.
- **AI-3**: The AI opens with a sensible build order: power is kept ahead of drain, a barracks and a second refinery come early, and it never queues what it cannot afford or is not allowed to build.
- **AI-4**: The AI adapts its army to what it has seen: enemy air makes it build anti-air; enemy armour makes it build anti-armour.
- **AI-5**: The AI keeps its haulers working: it replaces lost haulers and re-tasks idle ones.
- **AI-6**: The AI scouts the enemy, remembers what it saw (including buildings), and retreats scouts from armed enemies.
- **AI-7**: The AI defends: enemy armed units near its buildings pull its army to the threat.
- **AI-8**: The AI attacks in waves only when its army is clearly stronger than what it knows of the target, retreats a losing wave, and does not feed lone units into defences.
- **AI-9**: The AI repairs damaged buildings, salvages doomed ones, and fires a charged superweapon at a cluster of enemy buildings without hitting itself.
- **AI-10**: Difficulty levels differ only in reaction time, actions per minute and resource bonus.
- **HAR-1**: The headless harness plays complete AI-vs-AI matches deterministically: the same seeds and settings give identical results.
- **HAR-2**: The harness detects bad behaviour: idle haulers, stuck units, power outages left unfixed, hoarded credits, an undefended base, and suicidal attacks; a healthy AI stays under the thresholds.
- **HAR-3**: Across seeds and map sides, the Hard AI beats the Easy AI, and the Hard AI beats the Medium AI more often than not.
- **AI-11**: The AI attacks as one body: a wave sets out only once most of its army (three in four) has gathered at its staging point, or after about 45 seconds of waiting; it advances in short hops to a point just ahead of its centre, closing up first when strung out, so fast units wait for slow ones; and reinforcements gather at the staging point and join the wave in groups (at least three, or a quarter of the wave), never one at a time. Only the scout travels alone.
- **AI-17**: The AI scouts early and reliably. Its scout order comes before building and production orders, so the command budget never drops it; the scout leaves the army so no army order calls it back; without a scout unit it sends its fastest unit that is not a hero; and it goes around the enemy's front (to a flank point about halfway, alternating sides between trips) before heading for the enemy start. In headless Medium-against-Medium matches on every map, from both sides, every AI has found an enemy building by minute 4. Issue #104.
- **AI-18**: The AI works both of its home fields. It builds its second refinery beside the home field that has none yet (and later refineries beside the next field without one), wants three haulers a refinery (at most eight), sends each idle or new hauler to the field with the fewest haulers among those with one of its refineries beside them and no enemy army on them, and moves a hauler over when one field has two or more more than another; a hauler otherwise stays on its field (ECO-8), so the AI reassigns a hauler only when it is new, idle, or the split is uneven. In headless Medium-against-Medium matches on every map, from both sides, between minutes 5 and 6 each home field is worked by at least two haulers. Issue #112.
