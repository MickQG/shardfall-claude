# 005 Combat and rosters (milestone 5)

- **CMB-1**: Damage is the weapon's base damage times the damage-type percentage against the target's armor class, rounded down, never below 1.
- **CMB-2**: A weapon can hit air targets only if it is anti-air and ground targets only if it is anti-ground.
- **CMB-3**: A shooter fires only at targets within weapon range and never faster than the weapon cooldown.
- **CMB-4**: Idle units and defences automatically acquire visible enemies in range; a unit on a plain move order neither fires nor diverts.
- **CMB-5**: An attack order chases and kills its target; enemies on tiles the owner cannot see cannot be targeted.
- **CMB-6**: An attack-move engages enemies met on the way and then continues to its destination.
- **CMB-7**: Projectiles with flight time land later; splash damage hits enemy ground units near the impact point with falloff and spares friendly units.
- **CMB-8**: Units at zero hit points are removed and their attackers drop them; destroyed buildings free their tiles.
- **CMB-9**: Defence structures fire automatically and go offline while their owner is on low power.
- **CMB-10**: Units with regeneration heal when they have not taken damage for five seconds.
- **CMB-11**: Air units fly over blocked terrain and do not collide with ground units.
- **CMB-12**: A player with no buildings is defeated; the last player standing wins; the snapshot reports when the game is over and who won.
- **CMB-13**: A superweapon charges only while powered, fires at a chosen point after a delay, damages everything in its radius with falloff, and then recharges.
- **ROS-1**: Every building and unit of both factions is reachable through the tech tree from the headquarters, and all prerequisites and producers exist.
- **ROS-2**: Each faction can counter armour, infantry and air: it has an anti-armour weapon, an anti-air weapon, and artillery that outranges its own defences.
- **ROS-3**: At equal cost, like-for-like fights between the factions (rifle infantry, scouts, anti-armour infantry, tanks, and a mixed early-to-mid army) end within 20% of even on open ground, averaged over two army sizes and both starting sides; no faction out-trades the other across its core roster.
- **CMB-14**: Units cannot fire through buildings: a direct-fire shot needs a line to its target that crosses no building other than the target itself; a unit with its target in range but blocked moves for a clear shot, and while picking targets it passes over blocked ones. Lobbed weapons (artillery), aircraft, and shots at aircraft fire over buildings. Turrets are unaffected.
- **HERO-1**: The Aegis hero, the Paladin, replaces the Aegis Gunship: a giant four-legged assault walker built at the Motor Pool once a Tech Lab stands, with 3000 hit points (more than five Bulwark Tanks), that repairs itself out of combat and whose twin cannons and missile pods hit both ground and air. A player can have only one at a time, alive or in production: the sidebar shows "Only one at a time" while it is taken, the simulation refuses a second, and the AI fields one whenever it can afford it, never two. Its 3D model is about twice the size of a tank: four heavy reverse-jointed legs stepping in a diagonal trot under a broad armoured hull that turns to its target, gold trim, a glowing visor, a missile pod on each shoulder and twin cannons beneath.
- **HERO-2**: The Vrax hero, the Dreadstrider, replaces the Wasp: a towering six-legged bio-walker built at the Brood Pit once an Evolution Chamber stands, a match for the Paladin in toughness (3000 hit points) and firepower, that regenerates and whose underslung acid cannon hits ground and air. Like the Paladin it is one at a time, alive or in production, for players and the AI alike. With both heroes in, neither faction fields aircraft; the rules for aircraft stay in force for any that may come. Its 3D model is a carapace carried high on six long, splayed, jointed legs, with bone plates and spines along its back, glowing acid vents, a low armoured head and an acid cannon on a swivelling mount beneath it.
- **HERO-3**: A hero dies dramatically: internal blasts ripple through it for over a second, then a great final blast throws two shockwaves and a shower of debris, the wreck burns and smokes for several seconds and the camera shakes hard; a Vrax hero bursts in acid and ichor rather than fire.
