# 030 The AI against a steamroll (milestone 30)

A competent player beat the Hard AI by default with a steamroll: out-producing it (three shard fields with three or four haulers each, a chain of buildings toward the middle fields, four or five barracks and as many factories), massing rifle and anti-armour squads around crushers (Bulwark Tanks or Brutes), and winning with one attack in which the crushers flatten the AI's infantry squads. The AI fed that army with early waves, stopped at eight haulers, and answered armour with anti-armour squads that crushers run over: at equal cost in the open-ground battle harness a crusher stack beat the army the AI built against it in nearly every pairing. The fix is in how the AI grows, what it builds and how it moves; unit stats and the crush rule are unchanged, and Difficulty still changes only reaction time, orders per decision and shard bonus (AI-10). Issues #174 to #177 and #181.

- **HAR-5**: The harness has a steamroll opponent, for measuring only: it is not a personality, Random never draws it and the menu does not offer it. It plays the build the game's owner beats the Hard AI with, at Hard's reaction time and orders per decision and with no shard bonus (a human player gets none). Opening: power as needed, a refinery at its second home field, a barracks training rifle squads until its first factory stands, then from the factory one crusher and then haulers. After that its barracks train anti-armour and rifle squads (about 55% anti-armour) and its factories crushers, and it grows to five barracks and five factories. It takes a third field, building toward it when it is out of placement reach, with four haulers at a field of thirty shard tiles or more and three at a smaller one. It builds no turret, lab, hero, artillery, scout unit or superweapon. It keeps its whole army at home, defending, until the army is worth 14 000 credits and holds at least six crushers; then it attacks once with everything, as one body with crush orders (AI-11, BAL-5), never retreats, sends later units after the army in groups, and masses again if the attack is wiped out. The same seeds and settings give the same game (HAR-1), and an AI that does not face it plays exactly as before. A match series plays the Hard AI of each personality against it: 8 seeds, Aegis against Vrax both ways round, from both sides, 96 games of up to 20 minutes. Baseline before the changes of this spec: the Hard AI won 19 of the 96 (as Raider 10 of 32, Balanced 5, Doomsday 4; as Aegis none of 48, as Vrax 19 of 48); the opponent's first attack set out at about minute 6 with an army worth about 19 500 credits, and its army peaked at about twice the value of the AI's.
- **AI-21**: Every AI keeps a standing scout, so it knows what the enemy is building. Once the building that trains its scout unit stands (Scout Buggy at the Vehicle Factory, Stalker at the Brood Pit), it trains one, ahead of other combat units from that building, and trains a replacement when it has none: no sooner than 30 s after a loss, or 60 s if the lost scout lived less than a minute, so it does not feed scouts to the same guns. The standing scout is never part of the army: it joins no wave, gathering or base defence, and the AI trains no second scout unit while it lives. It watches rather than fights. It tours observation points round the enemy base, but not its front, where the enemy gathers and its waves set out; the points are 9 tiles out from the enemy buildings it knows (from the enemy start, read off the symmetric map, until it knows one). It goes to and fro along them, moving on when it reaches one, and a replacement starts on the other side. It keeps off ground within 8 tiles of enemy buildings and near armed enemy units it saw in the last 30 s, and moves along a way round that ground in hops of at most 4 tiles, so it decides again before it can drive into guns it has not seen; if no point can be reached that way it goes to its staging point. Whatever its health, when an armed enemy unit comes within 10 tiles of it (or within that unit's gun range plus 2 tiles, if that is farther) or a defence within 8, it flees 8 tiles straight away from the armed enemies it sees, and then tours the other way round. Until it has a scout unit the AI scouts as before with its fastest unit (AI-17), which then rejoins the army; every AI still finds an enemy building by minute 4 (AI-17), and Raider still scouts first (AI-14). The steamroll opponent keeps no standing scout (HAR-5). Difficulty changes none of this beyond reaction time and orders per decision (AI-10): a slower AI moves in hops and stops between them. Measured in headless matches on every map, from both sides, against the same matches with the old scouting (12 games each): at Hard the AI loses 1.0 scouts a game (was 2.7), its standing scout is alive at least 95% of the time, and it has seen 51% of the enemy army's value within the last minute (was 44%); at Medium it loses 1.1 (was 3.1), the scout is alive about 96% of the time, and it has seen 45% (was 40%). The match test requires, with the standing scout on: at Hard at most 2 scouts lost per AI per game, a standing scout alive at least 85% of the time and coverage of at least 40%; at Medium at most 2.5 lost, alive at least 80% and coverage of at least 36%.
- **AI-22**: The AI turns its income into army as fast as a strong player does: production buildings come first. Measured against the steamroll opponent (HAR-5) before this change, the Hard AI earned more than the opponent with fewer haulers, but at the opponent's first attack it had four production buildings to the opponent's six, its second factory stood two minutes later (minute 4.5 against 2.5), and about 20 000 credits sat banked or paid into queues that had not built yet; its headquarters spent the time on its lab, turrets and extra power. Build order: after its first barracks, second refinery and first factory (AI-3) the AI builds a second factory, a second barracks and a third factory before its lab, any turret or its superweapon, with power only as its margin needs (AI-3). From then on, whenever every barracks and every factory it has is busy and it has more than 1 500 credits banked, its next building is another barracks or factory, whichever it has fewer of (a factory on a tie), up to five of each, ahead of everything but power. Turrets wait until it has six production buildings or its base has been attacked; its lab waits until it has five production buildings. Personalities keep their character: Doomsday builds its lab straight after its second factory (amending AI-15, which had it after the first) and so still orders it before Balanced does; Raider builds no lab, turret or superweapon during its opening (AI-14). Queues stay shallow, so credits are not locked in orders that will not start soon: at most two units deep in a building, three with more than 3 000 credits banked (was up to five), and at most two artillery pieces in production at once. Fields and haulers are unchanged (AI-18: three refineries and eight haulers at most): taking further fields was tried and made the AI weaker, because its income already exceeded what it could spend and each hauler takes a factory's time. Difficulty changes none of this beyond reaction time, orders per decision and shard bonus (AI-10). The steamroll opponent keeps its own build (HAR-5). Measured (thresholds set from the baseline in issue #181): in the steamroll series, at the opponent's first attack the Hard AI has at least as many production buildings as the opponent at the median and less banked than before; in Medium-against-Medium matches on every map an AI has its second factory by minute 3.5 and at least six production buildings by minute 6 at the median; hoarding and the other harness violations stay under their thresholds (HAR-2), match length stays in its target (HAR-4), and faction balance and the difficulty order hold (ROS-4, HAR-3).
