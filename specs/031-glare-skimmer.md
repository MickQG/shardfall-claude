# 031 Glare Skimmer (milestone 31)

Issue #191: a Vrax long-range anti-armour hover tank, so the Vrax can out-range and kite massed Bulwark Tanks the way Bulwarks kite Brutes. Agreed in a design session on 2026-11-16; the stats are a first draft, tuned in CI. The network version goes to 10, since the simulation gains a unit and a rule.

- **SKM-1**: The Glare Skimmer is a Vrax unit trained at the Brood Pit with no further prerequisite (tier 2, as the Brute): heavy armour, 400 hit points, speed 40, cost 950, build time 520 ticks, vision 9, and it regenerates (2) like the other Vrax. Its weapon is a lance (SKM-2) of 60 damage every 40 ticks at range 8, which hits ground only; it is not a hero, not a harvester and not a crusher. Its role is anti-armour: the unit sheet calls it Anti-armour, Very tough, Average speed, Long range, cannot hit air, Regenerates. Hovering changes no terrain rule: it moves, collides and paths like any ground unit, and is hit as one.
- **SKM-2**: A new damage type, `lance`, does 160% to heavy armour, 50% to light armour, 60% to structures and nothing to aircraft. The lance hits at once (no flight time) and fires along a direct line, so buildings block it (CMB-14).
- **SKM-3**: Fire on the move is a unit flag (`firesMoving`) apart from crushing. A unit with it keeps shooting at enemies in range while it carries out a plain move order, without stopping or leaving its path, as crushers do (BAL-11, unchanged); other units still hold fire while moving (CMB-4). The Skimmer has the flag and crushes nothing: enemy squads it drives through are pushed aside as by any other unit and never flattened.
- **SKM-4**: The Skimmer looks like an original Vrax design. In the 3D view it is a long, flattened organic hull, like a ray's body, floating about 0.3 tiles above the ground (its lowest point within 0.02 of that), so its shadow lies on the ground apart from it (VOX-8); its underside glows; team-coloured carapace, finned flanks, a blunt head with glowing eyes, a bone tail. A long lance emitter (a ribbed bone spine with glowing coils and a glowing tip) sits on a round mount on its back and turns all round to its target, clear of the hull (AIM-2); shots leave its tip. The hull bobs gently and banks a little in turns; when it dies the hulk drops to the ground before it burns. When it fires, a continuous beam is held from the tip to the target for 0.42 s, flickering with a white-hot core, with a flash and sparks where it hits. The classic view draws a simple voxel model whose lowest voxel is well above the ground (a detached shadow, VOX-8), and the lance as a held beam with a flash at the target.
- **SKM-5**: The Skimmer's sounds are procedural like the rest (AUD-5, AUD-6): moving Skimmers make a hover hum (a low, slowly throbbing drone, its own movement class beside vehicles, walkers, infantry and swarms, low-passed well below the hiss band, AUD-8), and each lance shot plays a beam tone that rises in pitch over the held beam, with a short attack and a tail. Its acknowledgement sound is its own (AUD-7).
- **SKM-6**: The AI (every difficulty and personality) trains Skimmers as its main anti-armour unit when the armed enemy units it has seen in the last two minutes are mostly heavy armour by cost (the credits' worth of heavy-armoured ones above half of all of them). Then the Skimmer is in its Brood Pit's mix as an anti-armour unit, and, unless the enemy is infantry-heavy by count (more than 45% squads, BAL-6, which crushers answer), the Pit trains a Skimmer in place of whatever else its mix would train there, except artillery. Its standing scout, its hero, its artillery floor and any artillery the mix wants come first, so AI-19 still holds. Skimmers count as anti-armour in the mix, so fewer Spitters are needed. Otherwise it trains no Skimmers. An AI rule, `skimmers`, turns this off for measurement (HAR-6).
