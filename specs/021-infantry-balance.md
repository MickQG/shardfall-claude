# 021 Infantry balance: crushing, slower heroes, straight shells, elite shots (milestone 21)

Massed anti-armour infantry (Rocketeers, Spitters) used to win by sheer numbers: at equal cost they out-traded tanks about three to one, and tanks had no answer to a blob of infantry. Heavy vehicles now crush infantry, infantry deals less damage, and a mixed army beats infantry spam. Issue #89.

- **BAL-1**: Heavy vehicles crush infantry. The Bulwark Tank, the Brute, the Paladin and the Dreadstrider are crushers; harvesters and artillery are not. When a crusher moves (its position changes this tick: standing still, firing or turning in place does not count), every enemy infantry squad (Rifleman, Rocketeer, Skitterer, Spitter) whose centre lies inside the crusher's body dies at once, whole squad. The crusher earns the squad's remaining hit points as veterancy experience (VET-1). A player's own squads are never crushed; they are pushed aside as before. Enemy squads give way to a crusher, but a crusher is not pushed back by enemy squads. Crushing happens on any move and needs no command.
- **BAL-2**: A crushed squad is flattened into the ground rather than toppling. In the 3D view its figures squash flat, darken and sink away; Aegis infantry leave a puff of dirt and dust and a scuffed mark, Vrax infantry a dark ichor splat and a fading pool. In the classic view it leaves a flat puff and a mark. A crushed squad makes a short crunch, not an explosion. The death event says it was crushed.
- **BAL-3**: Infantry deals less damage, and tanks stay weak against light armour. Rocketeer 38 (was 40), Spitter 30 (was 31), Rifleman 7 (was 8), Skitterer 7 (was 8). `rocket` damage, fired by Rocketeers, Spitters, tanks and heroes, does 45% to light armour (was 60%), so anti-armour infantry and tanks both do much less to infantry. The Skitterer and the Stalker bite (`bite`: light 110%, heavy 40%, structure 50%, air 0%), so they are weak against tanks, as Riflemen are. The Brute has 620 hit points (was 710) and its `claw` does 80% to heavy armour (was 70%), keeping tank-against-Brute fights even (ROS-3).
- **BAL-4**: Tank and hero shells fly straight. The Bulwark Tank's shell, the Paladin's shell and the Dreadstrider's acid bolt travel flat and straight from the muzzle to the target in both renderers, and faster: tank 3 ticks (was 4), Paladin 5 (was 8), Dreadstrider 5 (was 9). Artillery (Hammer, Lobber) still lobs on a high arc, and infantry rockets and acid keep their small arc.
- **BAL-5**: The AI crushes, but shoots first. AI crushers fight with their guns like any other unit; each healthy AI crusher (at least 30% of its hit points) that sees an enemy it can crush (BAL-1, BAL-10) close by, within 6 tiles (inside the gun range of every crusher that has a gun), drives straight through it to a point 1.5 tiles beyond, and keeps firing on the way (BAL-11). Crush orders override that tick's wave orders for the same units and always get a share of the AI's command budget. AI infantry does not dodge crushers.
- **BAL-6**: Against an infantry-heavy enemy (more than 45% of the armed enemy units it has seen are infantry squads), the AI builds more tanks and rifle infantry and fewer anti-armour units.
- **BAL-7**: Elite shots. Shots fired by a unit at the top veterancy rank (rank 3, VET-1) glow violet instead of their weapon's own colour, in both renderers, so an elite unit is visible in a firefight. The shot event says when it came from an elite unit.
- **BAL-8**: Heroes are slower, for weight. The Paladin and the Dreadstrider move at 20 (was 32; slower than artillery, "Slow" on the unit sheet), and turn slowly (HERO-5). Their gait is tied to the distance walked, so their stride slows with them rather than shuffling.
- **BAL-10**: Heroes crush vehicles too. The Paladin and the Dreadstrider crush any enemy ground unit except a hero: infantry squads, tanks, Brutes, artillery, harvesters and scouts. Aircraft and buildings are never crushed, and heroes never crush each other. The rules of BAL-1 apply: the hero must be moving, the victim dies at once when its centre lies inside the hero's body, and the hero earns the victim's remaining hit points as experience and takes no damage. A vehicle the hero can crush is not pushed aside by it and does not push it back, so the hero walks straight over it. A crushed vehicle or walker also bursts (a big death in its event, a small explosion in the 3D view and a louder crunch). The Bulwark Tank and the Brute still crush infantry squads only.
- **BAL-11**: Crushers fire on the move. A crusher with a move order keeps shooting at enemies in range while it drives, without stopping or leaving its path; other units still hold fire while moving.
- **BAL-9**: Balance targets, checked in the headless battle harness, with both sides using the AI's crush orders (BAL-5) and averaged over two army sizes and both starting sides. At equal cost: a pure Rocketeer army loses to a mixed tank-and-rifle Aegis army, and a pure Spitter army loses to a mixed Brute-and-Skitterer Vrax army, each by at least 25% of the army's value, with crushers running squads over. Pure anti-armour infantry and pure crushers (Rocketeers and Spitters against Bulwark Tanks and Brutes) trade about evenly: on average neither side wins by more than 15%, with the crushers destroying at least 60% of the infantry's value in every pairing (since BAL-12 made tanks faster than infantry; before, the infantry won narrowly). ROS-3 and ROS-4 still hold.
- **BAL-12**: Tanks outpace infantry. Unit speeds (256 = one tile a tick) fall in a clear order: scouts (Scout Buggy 70, Stalker 92), then the Skitterer swarm (50, was 60), then tanks (Bulwark Tank 42, was 34; Brute 38, was 33), then line infantry (Rifleman 28, was 38; Rocketeer 27, was 34; Spitter 30, was 40), then artillery (Hammer 24, Lobber 26), then heroes (20, BAL-8); haulers (Shard Hauler 30, Gorger 34) are unchanged. Every tank is at least a quarter faster than every infantry squad except the Skitterer, and slower than both scouts. Faster tanks run squads over more often, so BAL-9 now expects an even trade between anti-armour infantry and crushers rather than a narrow infantry win; ROS-3, ROS-4 and HAR-3 still hold. Issue #101.
- **BAL-14**: The Vrax Hive is a little slower, so it no longer counters the Aegis Union faster than they can answer: Skitterer 47 (was 50), Spitter 29 (was 30), Stalker 86 (was 92), Lobber 25 (was 26); the Brute, Gorger and Dreadstrider are unchanged. A slower Skitterer swarm would lose the rifle fight outright (ROS-3), so each Skitterer gets 77 hit points (was 74). The BAL-12 speed order and every balance target (ROS-3, ROS-4, BAL-9, BAL-13, HAR-3) still hold. Issue #109.
- **BAL-15**: Heroes are durable meat-shield tanks rather than damage dealers: the Paladin and the Dreadstrider have 4500 hit points (was 3000) and hit for 90 (was 110; still more than twice a Bulwark Tank shell, HERO-4); their cost, speed and armour are unchanged. Checked in the headless battle harness: a hero standing in front of a line of artillery keeps the artillery almost unharmed (at most 10% of its value lost) against attackers that destroy an unshielded line; and a lone hero attacked by a fixed force (nine tanks of the other faction, or eighteen anti-armour squads) lasts at least a fifth longer than it did before in every pairing and about 30% longer on average, yet still falls to that force (more hit points would let a lone hero beat it outright). Issue #111.
- **BAL-13**: Defences are worth building. The Pulse Turret has 900 hit points (was 500) and fires a pulse beam (`beam`: full damage to every armour) of 26 (was 22 `pierce`); the Spine Spire has 700 hit points (was 450). Checked in the headless harness, a lone powered defence with its headquarters behind it, attacked across open ground by one kind of the enemy's early units (Pulse Turret: Skitterers, Spitters, Brutes; Spine Spire: Riflemen, Rocketeers, Bulwark Tanks): it still stands against attackers costing twice its cost, and it falls to attackers costing four times its cost. Artillery still outranges both (ROS-2). Issue #100.
