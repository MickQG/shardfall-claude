# 013 Unit info (milestone 13)

A main-menu panel that tells a new player, in plain words, what each unit of the chosen faction is for. Everything shown is derived from the unit definitions and the damage table, so it stays correct when balance changes. Buildings, defences, enemy inspection and opening the panel in game are out of scope.

- **INF-1**: The main menu has an info button that opens a panel (the unit sheet) on the faction currently picked on the menu, with one entry per unit kind of that faction; tabs for both factions switch between them, and the sheet closes with its close button, a click outside it or `Escape`.
- **INF-2**: Each card shows the unit's name, its cost in credits, a role line (Harvester, Infantry, Anti-armour, Scout, Tank, Artillery, Air, Raider) and words rather than numbers for: toughness (Fragile under 150 HP, Sturdy 150 to 399, Very tough 400 or more), speed (Slow below 30, Average below 50, Fast otherwise, in speed units), range (Melee under 2 tiles, Short under 5, Medium under 8, Long otherwise), whether it can hit air, and a "Regenerates" tag for units that heal.
- **INF-3**: "Strong against" lists the armour classes its weapon deals 100% or more against and can hit, each with the opposing faction's unit kinds of that class ("Buildings" for structures); a unit without a weapon says it cannot attack.
- **INF-4**: "Weak against" lists the opposing faction's unit kinds whose weapon can hit this unit and deals 100% or more against its armour class.
- **INF-5**: The card contents come from a pure function of the unit definitions and damage table, testable without a browser.
- **INF-6**: The unit sheet lists the faction's units, each with a picture of its in-game 3D model, its name, role and cost; the first is chosen when the sheet opens, and clicking an entry or Up and Down choose another (Left and Right switch faction). A detail pane shows the chosen unit's 3D model in its owner's colours on a plinth, turning slowly and moving as in a match: it travels a weaving course at its match speed with the game's own gait, hull turning, body motion and squad movement, comes to a stop, then works. Armed units fire at their match rate with the game's muzzle flashes and shots; harvesters, which have no weapon, mine (the cutter turns or the maw chomps, and the load fills) and then unload instead. It is held still when the system asks for reduced motion; beside it the INF-2..4 contents, the building that trains it and the buildings it needs. Squads show all their figures.
- **INF-7**: The detail pane shows bars and numbers for health, speed, range (tiles) and firepower (damage per second: damage times squad figures times 20 over the weapon cooldown, 0 when unarmed). Each bar is filled by the square root of the value's share of the largest value of that stat across both factions, so small units still show; the numbers come from a pure function of the unit definitions.
- **INF-8**: The 3D previews load only when the sheet first opens, so the menu itself loads no 3D code; without WebGL the sheet still works, with a note in place of the model and no pictures.
