# 023 AI personalities (milestone 23)

An AI opponent plays one of three personalities, so games against it stop converging. A personality is chosen per match, independently of Difficulty, and changes only what the AI chooses to do: AI-2 (no vision cheat) and AI-10 (what Difficulty may change) still hold. Issue #98.

- **AI-12**: Every AI opponent plays one personality for the whole match: Raider, Balanced or Doomsday. Every Difficulty works with every personality. The personality is part of the match settings, so the same seeds and settings give the same game (HAR-1).
- **AI-13**: Balanced plays as the AI did before personalities existed (AI-1..11).
- **AI-14**: Raider opens with early pressure on the economy. It scouts first, fields more fast and cheap units, builds no defences and no tech (lab, superweapon) during its opening, and aims its attacks at the nearest part of the enemy economy it knows: a refinery or extractor, a hauler seen lately, or, unseen, the shard field nearest the enemy start (the map shows every player the fields). Across the harness seeds, Medium against a Medium Balanced AI, its first attack sets out before minute 4 of game time in every game, and more than half of its attacks in the first eight minutes are aimed at the economy. Its attacks still gather as a group (AI-11).
- **AI-15**: Doomsday builds towards its superweapon first: it saves the superweapon's full price, holds with at most two defences until it stands, adds a second barracks and factory only with money to spare, and keeps its army at home. Across the harness seeds its superweapon building stands earlier than Balanced's and Raider's in every game. Once the superweapon is charged, its whole army sets out at once and the weapon holds fire (for up to 90 seconds) until the army is close: it strikes an enemy army standing in the way, or the defences at the army's objective, or the objective itself when nothing there is known, and sends the army straight into the struck area. The army does not retreat while the strike is charged or has just landed. Over the harness games with a strike, the median time from the first strike until three or more of its units are in the struck area is ten seconds or less.
- **AI-16**: Raider and Doomsday commit to their plan, then adapt: Raider plays as Balanced once three of its attacks have been beaten back or eight minutes have passed; Doomsday plays as Balanced if its superweapon building is destroyed, still rebuilding it first when it can.
- **MENU-5**: The Single player panel has a Personality control (Random, Raider, Balanced, Doomsday) beside the opponent Difficulty. Random is the default; the choice is remembered on the device. Random is drawn from the game seed, so it is deterministic.
- **SCR-4**: The result screen of a game against the AI names the opponent's personality and difficulty ("Your opponent played Doomsday on Hard."). While the game runs the player is never shown it, even when the menu choice was Random.
- **HAR-4**: An on-demand harness script, `npm run ai:length` (not part of `npm test` or CI), plays each personality against a Balanced AI, Medium against Medium, on every map from both sides and with both factions, and reports the median, shortest and longest match length and whether the median meets the target of 12 to 20 minutes.
