# 022 Aim and clean models (milestone 22)

Shots leave the barrel along the line it points, and moving parts never cut through each other. Raised on PR #90 (issue #89): the Paladin's shots in the unit sheet flew off at an angle to its cannons, and its hull twisted over its legs, cutting into them and hanging in mid air.

- **AIM-1**: Every shot goes where its gun points, in a match and in the unit sheet's preview. A lone walker (a hero, a Brute, a Lobber, a Stalker) turns its whole body to face its target whenever it shoots, walking or not, and a turret turns relative to the body it sits on (not to the direction of travel), so a turned body and its turret never add up to twice the angle. A shot leaves only once the gun itself (the body's facing plus the turret's turn, within the turret's reach) is on target, or after the usual short wait. In the preview each shot flies out along its barrel's line to the weapon's range.
- **AIM-2**: Moving parts never cut into each other or the ground. As a unit aims all round, walks through a full stride, steers, leans, rolls, recoils, lunges, strikes, kneels or unloads, no part pushes into another by more than 0.03 tiles beyond how they meet at rest; the one exception is a barrel recoiling into its own mount, and it never comes out of the mount's back. At rest every unit stands on the ground: no foot, wheel or track is sunk into it or floats above it by more than 0.02 tiles. The Paladin's hull rides rigidly on its four legs, which stand just clear of its sides; only the cannon cradle under its nose swivels, at most 0.2 rad either way, and the walker turns to its target for the rest. The Dreadstrider's cannon swivels at most 0.5 rad; front wheels steer at most 0.3 rad.
- **AIM-3**: Shots do not all land on one point. Each drawn shot (tracer, shell, rocket, spit or spine, with its impact flash) ends at its own point on the target, scattered across the target's body around the aim point: within about 60% of a unit's radius, or of a building's half-width and half-depth, and over part of its height. A shot at a building ends on the face toward the shooter, where its line to that point first meets the building's footprint, so shots walk across the visible wall instead of vanishing inside the model. The point is never off the target and never below the ground. Successive shots from the same shooter land on different points, and each member of a squad has its own. The scatter is drawn only: damage, timing and the simulation are unchanged, and it is the same for a given shooter and shot number rather than random per frame, so a replayed fight looks the same. Melee attacks and superweapon strikes are not scattered, and an area blast (artillery) shifts by no more than a fifth of a tile, so its mark stays where the damage is. Guns still turn to the middle of the target (AIM-1); only where the shot ends changes.
