# 025 Front-end and HUD (milestone 25)

A new look for the menu and the in-match HUD: a live faction scene behind the menu, an original wordmark, a condensed typeface already on the player's device, a skin for each faction, model pictures on the build buttons, and a How to play page. Everything is drawn in code; no image, font or audio file is added. Issue #142.

- **MENU-8**: The rail's title is an original wordmark drawn in code as inline SVG: a geometric shard emblem split by a fracture into two offset pieces, beside the name SHARDFALL set in the heading typeface (MENU-9) in flat colours, with a hairline fracture cutting through the letters. It has no bevel, chrome gradient or glow. Its accessible name is "Shardfall". No image or font file is added for it.
- **MENU-9**: Menu and HUD text use typefaces already on the player's device. Headings and labels use a condensed face: Bahnschrift, then DIN Condensed, DIN Alternate, Roboto Condensed, Arial Narrow, then the system sans-serif. Body text uses Bahnschrift, then Segoe UI, then the system sans-serif. The build contains no font files.
- **MENU-10**: In the 3D view a live scene sits behind the menu. It shows the picked faction's hero and three other units of that faction standing in front of the faction's HQ on a patch of ground, lit like a match (late-afternoon sun, cool ambient light, bloom), using the in-game models with render-only idle motion. The camera dollies slowly back and forth; one pass takes 20 to 30 seconds. Picking the other faction crossfades to that faction's scene within 0.6 s. On Low quality, or when the system asks for reduced motion, the scene is drawn once and holds still. It pauses while the unit sheet is open. It stops drawing and releases its WebGL context when the menu closes. Where the classic view is used (`?renderer=classic`, no WebGL2, or software rendering), the drifting backdrop is shown instead.
- **MENU-11**: The menu panel is a translucent column with a maximum width. On screens 1600 px and wider the scene's hero stands clear of the panel, to its right. On narrower screens the scene shows through the panel's translucent backing. Panel text stays readable (UI-21).
- **MENU-12**: The menu wears the picked faction's skin (UI-16), including its accents, frames and START button, and switches with the pick. Each faction card always keeps its own faction's frame, whichever faction is picked: the Aegis card stays angular with chamfered corners, and the Vrax card stays rounded.
- **MENU-13**: Panels change with a short fade and slide of at most 250 ms, and START fades the menu to black before the game loads. With reduced motion, panels change instantly and START does not fade.
- **MENU-14**: The rail has five entries, in this order: Single player, Multiplayer, Units, How to play, Settings. It has no help text. How to play has two sections. Controls is a table of every mouse and key binding (UI-2). Basics is six short points: shards and haulers make credits; the power grid; building up the tiers; heroes; superweapons; how you win. The table and the points come from the same source as the F1 overlay (UI-18).
- **MENU-15**: The rail has no Music button. Settings has a Sound group (Mute all; Master volume) and a Music group (Music on or off; Music volume). Changes are saved on the device at once, and the next game uses them. M toggles Mute all, in the menu and in a match. Music off silences the menu score and the in-match music, but not sound effects. Music volume scales only the music. Settings saved before this change keep their mute and volume (as Mute all and Master volume), with Music on at the default music volume.
- **UI-16**: In a match the HUD wears the player's faction skin: the sidebar, minimap frame, selection info panel, top bar, superweapon countdown and toasts. Aegis has angular frames with chamfered corners, brushed-steel gradients and cyan accents. Vrax has rounded, asymmetric chitin frames with amber-green accents and a faint inner glow. The skins are drawn with CSS and inline SVG only. Online, each player sees their own faction's skin.
- **UI-17**: Each build and train button in the sidebar shows a picture of that unit or building, drawn from its in-game 3D model in the player's team colour, beside its name and cost. The pictures are drawn once while the game loads, never per frame. In the classic view the buttons stay text only.
- **UI-18**: The top bar shows "F1 Help", plus the game speed in single-player or the room code online. It lists no other keys. F1 opens an overlay with the Controls table from How to play (MENU-14), and F1 or Esc closes it. Esc closes the overlay first, before doing anything else. The browser's own F1 help never opens. The game keeps running while the overlay is open.
- **UI-19**: The loading screen (UI-12) wears the player's faction skin and shows the wordmark (MENU-8) and one tip taken from the Basics points (MENU-14).
- **UI-20**: The post-game score screen wears the player's faction skin and the heading typeface. Its content (SCR-1..5) is unchanged.
- **UI-21**: In both skins, every piece of menu and HUD text has a contrast ratio against the colour behind it of at least 4.5:1, or 3:1 for headings of 18 px and up. This is checked from the skin's colour tokens.
